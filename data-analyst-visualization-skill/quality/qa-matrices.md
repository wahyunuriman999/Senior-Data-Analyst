# Layered QA Matrices
Perform mandatory checks before output:
- **DATA**: Row integrity, missingness, duplicates, join audit.
- **CALCULATION**: Formulas, denominators, currency, units.
- **VISUAL**: Suitability, zero-baselines (contextual), color encoding, accessibility.
- **DESIGN**: Alignment, density, hierarchy.
- **STORY**: Does the headline match the evidence?
