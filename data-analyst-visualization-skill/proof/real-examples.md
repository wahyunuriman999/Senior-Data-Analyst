# Real Examples (No Placeholders)
## Example 1: Variance Decomposition
**Context**: Revenue dropped by $500k.
**Analysis**: The engine isolated volume vs. price mix. Price remained flat, but volume in the EMEA segment dropped by 30% due to a specific product line stockout.
**Visual**: A Waterfall chart showing the $500k drop, explicitly labeling the EMEA product line as the -600k detractor, offset by a +100k gain in NA.

## Example 2: Cohort Retention
**Context**: SaaS churn.
**Analysis**: Monthly cohorts plotted on a retention matrix. The engine identifies that Month-3 retention for the Q2 cohorts is significantly lower than Q1.
**Visual**: A triangulated heatmap (Cohort Retention Matrix) with annotated structural breaks.
