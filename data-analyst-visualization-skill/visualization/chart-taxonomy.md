# Full Chart Taxonomy
You must utilize the appropriate chart from the apex catalog:
- **Distributions**: Ridgeline, ECDF, Beeswarm, Strip plot, Violin, Box.
- **Relationships**: Hexbin, Pair plot, Scatter with marginals.
- **Composition**: Marimekko, Sunburst, Waffle, 100% Stacked.
- **Flow**: Sankey, Alluvial, Chord, Flow Map.
- **Temporal/Project**: Gantt, Timeline, Candlestick, OHLC.
- **Geographic**: Hexbin map, Dot density, Choropleth.
- **Statistical**: Forest plot, Control charts (SPC).
