# Segmentation, Cohort & Funnel Analysis
- **Cohorts**: Align users by a common event (e.g., sign-up month) and track retention over normalized time (Month 0, 1, 2).
- **Funnels**: Measure step-to-step conversion. Diagnose the exact drop-off stage.
- **Segmentation**: Use clustering or categorical splits to find behavioral differences. Avoid segments that are too small to be statistically significant.
